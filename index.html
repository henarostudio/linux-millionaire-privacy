Linux Millionaire Privacy Policy

Developer / publisher: Henaro Studio
Privacy contact: henarostudio@mail.bg
Effective date: November 23, 2026
Public policy URL: https://henarostudio.github.io/linux-millionaire-privacy/

Linux Millionaire is an offline Linux, Bash, and system administration quiz game. It does not require an account or sign-in. Quiz questions are included in the app. The app does not provide multiplayer, chat, or user-generated content.

Information handled by the app

The app stores music and sound-effect on/off choices in local device preferences so those choices can be remembered. You can remove these audio preferences by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Player Statistics

The app stores gameplay history in a private SQLite database on your device to show your Player Statistics. Records include the day played, levels reached, questions attempted and whether answers were correct, question categories, lifelines used, active play time, and how games ended. The app does not upload these records or send them to Google or advertising partners.

Gameplay history has no automatic expiry. To delete it and reset your statistics, open Player Statistics, choose Clear statistics, and confirm. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app also removes the local history. Android’s standard cloud backup and device-to-device transfer exclude the folder where these records and their storage metadata are kept.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising and privacy choices. Google and any advertising partners selected in the final AdMob configuration may process advertising identifiers, device and account identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. An IP address may indicate an approximate location.

The advertising partners, processing purposes, and optional advertising features depend on the production AdMob configuration. Google and any participating advertising partners describe their data retention practices in their own privacy policies.

The app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check your privacy choices and show consent forms when required. When available, open Ad Privacy Options in Settings to review or change your choices. Consent choices can affect which ads are requested or shown, including whether ads are personalized. Google’s advertising and consent services use a network connection and may store advertising or consent information on your device. Test-ad builds can also contact Google services.

Google service information is available in the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. Additional partner information will be provided if production partners require it.

What the developer does not collect directly

The app does not ask users to submit a name, email address, payment information, or account credentials. Version 1 has no developer-operated account system, custom analytics service, crash-reporting service, backend, payment, or billing system. This does not limit data processed by the Google advertising and consent SDKs described above.

Children and audience

Linux Millionaire is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over. It is not specifically directed to children.

Data security, retention, and requests

Local audio preferences remain on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. The applicable third-party processing and retention practices are described by Google and any production advertising partners. Henaro Studio does not operate a user account or app-owned backend for this version.

For privacy questions or requests, contact henarostudio@mail.bg. Requests involving data held by Google or another advertising partner may need to be handled with that provider under its policies and procedures.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when a revised version takes effect.
